About KADIS ICT & Engineering Solutions

KADIS ICT & Engineering Solutions is an Abuja-based technology solutions and systems-integration company delivering ICT, digital, security, power, media and event technology, from equipment sourcing and supply to professional installation, integration, deployment and ongoing technical support.

We Supply. We Install. We Integrate. We Support. Started in 2020 with a simple idea: Abuja shouldn't have to juggle five different vendors for gadgets, security, solar and installations. One technology partner, one team, one number to call — backed by our own in-house engineers, not a call center.

We serve individuals, homes, offices, schools and businesses alike, there's no requirement too small, and we also handle corporate and bulk procurement for organizations.
