WHY GO SMART WITH KADIS

Built for Abuja homes, not showroom demos

Nigerian power and network conditions are part of the design brief, not an afterthought.

A lot of "smart home" gear sold online assumes stable power and a fast, uninterrupted internet connection. We design and install with local conditions in mind — battery and solar backup, offline fallback modes for locks and gates, and local support when a device needs a reset, not a call center abroad.

Every install starts with a real conversation about what you actually want automated — not a fixed package. A one-bedroom flat and a five-bedroom duplex don't need the same system, and our engineers scope it accordingly before any device is bought.
