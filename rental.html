What you'll need to rent

  • 1 A valid government-issued photo ID — NIN slip/card, driver's licence, international passport or voter's card.
  • 2 A passport photograph.
  • 3 A guarantor who signs the rental agreement with you.
  • 4 A refundable security deposit (or other agreed security), sized to the equipment value.
  • 5 A signed Equipment Rental Agreement covering the rental period and equipment condition.
Note: Higher-value equipment — laptops, cameras, networking gear, generators or engineering instruments — may need a larger deposit or additional documentation. We'll confirm exact requirements when you request a quote.
