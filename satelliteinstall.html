HOW EACH SERVICE WORKS

Satellite services, step by step

The exact process our engineers follow for every job, so you know what to expect before we arrive.

📡 GOtv Installation Services Process

From a first phone call to a fully activated decoder, here's how we get your GOtv connection running — and keep it running.

1
Site & Signal Assessment
We check your roof or wall access and confirm a clear line of sight to the satellite before any equipment goes up.
2
Dish Mounting & Alignment
The dish is mounted and precisely aligned for the strongest possible signal at your specific location.
3
Decoder Setup & Cabling
The decoder is connected, cabling is run neatly along walls or trunking, and every port is tested end-to-end.
4
Subscription Activation
Your GOtv smart card is registered and activated with the package you've chosen.
5
Signal Test & Channel Scan
A full channel scan is run and picture and sound quality confirmed on every connected TV point.
6
Customer Walkthrough
We show you how to read basic error prompts and reset the decoder yourself before we leave.

📺 SLTV (StarTimes) Installations

StarTimes sits on a different orbital position from GOtv, so alignment and setup are handled as their own dedicated process.

1
Site Assessment
We confirm your roof or mounting point has a clear, unobstructed view toward the StarTimes satellite position.
2
Dish Mounting & Fine Alignment
The dish is fixed and fine-tuned specifically for StarTimes' orbital position, distinct from GOtv or DStv alignment.
3
Decoder Installation
The decoder is connected to your TV, powered up and cabled through to every room that needs a feed.
4
Smart Card & Subscription Activation
Your smart card is registered and your chosen bouquet is activated on the spot.
5
Signal & Channel Testing
Every channel is scanned and checked for a clean, stable picture before the job is signed off.
6
Customer Walkthrough
We explain how to renew your subscription and what to do if the signal drops after weather changes.

🛰️ Starlink Installation & Configuration

Starlink doesn't point at one fixed satellite — it needs a clear view of open sky. That changes how the survey and setup are done.

1
Sky Visibility Survey
We check for obstructions — roof lines, trees, neighbouring buildings — since Starlink needs open sky rather than a fixed satellite point.
2
Mounting & Cable Routing
The dish is mounted on a roof, pole or wall bracket, with cabling routed cleanly indoors to the router.
3
Router & App Configuration
We set up the Starlink app on your phone and configure your Wi-Fi network name and password.
4
Speed & Obstruction Test
A live speed test is run and the app's obstruction map is reviewed to catch any blind spots early.
5
Network Optimization
Router placement is adjusted, and mesh extenders are added if your home needs wider coverage.
6
Ongoing Monitoring Support
We stay reachable if a firmware update or new obstruction later affects your connection.

🧰 Satellite Equipment Sourcing

Before any dish goes up, we make sure it's the right dish — genuine, compatible, and backed by a warranty.

1
Needs Consultation
We talk through which service — GOtv, SLTV or Starlink — and how many decoder or TV points you actually need.
2
Genuine Equipment Sourcing
Dishes, decoders, LNBs, cables and mounts are sourced only from authorized, genuine suppliers.
3
Compatibility Check
Equipment is matched to your building type, roof material and any existing setup already in place.
4
Warranty Registration
Every device is registered under its manufacturer or Kadis warranty before it's ever handed over.
5
Delivery & Handover
Equipment is delivered ready for installation — same-day within Abuja, or straight into our install team's hands.

🔧 Signal Troubleshooting & Repairs

No signal, weak signal or a decoder throwing error codes — here's how we track it down and fix it.

1
Remote Diagnosis
Describe the issue over phone or WhatsApp — no signal, weak signal, an error code — for an initial read on what's wrong.
2
On-Site Inspection
An engineer inspects dish alignment, cabling, the LNB and decoder ports for damage, rust or drift.
3
Realignment or Part Replacement
The dish is realigned, or a faulty cable, LNB or decoder is repaired or swapped out.
4
Signal Verification
A full signal strength and channel test is run before the job is considered done.
5
Prevention Advice
We explain what likely caused the fault — wind, rust, rodents, storm damage — and how to avoid a repeat.

🏢 Commercial Installation Services

Hotels, estates, schools and offices need satellite TV across many rooms at once — the process scales differently from a single home install.

1
Site Survey & Scoping
We assess the property to plan a distribution layout across every room or unit that needs a feed.
2
Centralized System Design
A headend or distribution plan is designed so one or a few dishes can serve many TV points at once.
3
Bulk Installation
Our engineering team installs across all units on an agreed schedule, with minimal disruption to daily operations.
4
Bulk Subscription & Configuration
Decoders and subscriptions are activated and configured together, room by room.
5
Full-System Testing
Every single point is individually tested before we sign off on the project.
6
Single Point of Contact
One dedicated contact manages support requests going forward, instead of juggling multiple technicians.
