WHAT TECHFORCE IS

The call-out arm of Kadis

Kadis sells and installs ICT equipment. TechForce is the side of the business that shows up after the sale — when a camera stops recording, a router needs reconfiguring, or an appliance just won't turn on. It's a standing team of technicians you can call the way you'd call a plumber or an electrician, except for technology.

Every technician works under Kadis directly, using the same tools and genuine parts we install on new jobs, so a repair carries the same standard as a fresh install.
